## 🌟 Top Account Planning Tools Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Strategic Account Management, Relationship Mapping, Whitespace Analysis & Revenue Growth Planning*

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Account Planning Tools**. These tools help sales and revenue teams create structured account plans, map stakeholder relationships, uncover whitespace opportunities, track objectives, and drive predictable growth in key accounts.

**Examples** include Altify, People.ai, Uptics, Membrain, Revegy, Strategic Relationship Solutions, Salesforce Account Plans, DemandFarm, ClientPoint, and Clari (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom relationship mapping, account hierarchy management, and open CRM-based planning workflows — ideal for sales teams, RevOps, and developers building transparent, data-owned account strategies.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- **[Frappe Framework + CRM](https://github.com/frappe/frappe)** and related apps for building fully custom account planning applications.
- **Relationship / Org-chart libraries** and visualization tools (D3.js-based org charts, GraphQL relationship graphs) that integrate with the CRMs above.
- **Open-source mutual action plan / playbook templates** and workflow engines (n8n, Node-RED, Temporal) for execution tracking.
- **LangGraph / AI agent frameworks** combined with CRM data for automated account research, risk detection, and next-best-action recommendations.
- Many community **account hierarchy**, **whitespace analysis notebooks**, and **stakeholder mapping** projects built on top of the major open CRMs.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Twenty** or **EspoCRM** / **SuiteCRM** with **n8n** (or Node-RED), **InfluxDB + Grafana** (for activity metrics), **Ollama** / local LLMs for research summaries, and custom relationship-graph visualizations for intelligent, self-hosted account planning platforms.
