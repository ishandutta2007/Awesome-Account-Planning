# Awesome-Account-Planning
## Top Account Planning Tools Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Strategic Account Management, Relationship Mapping, Whitespace Analysis & Revenue Growth Planning*
**Last updated: July 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Account Planning Tools**. These tools help sales and revenue teams create structured account plans, map stakeholder relationships, uncover whitespace opportunities, track objectives, and drive predictable growth in key accounts.

**Examples** include Altify, People.ai, Uptics, Membrain, Revegy, Strategic Relationship Solutions, Salesforce Account Plans, DemandFarm, ClientPoint, and Clari (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom relationship mapping, account hierarchy management, and open CRM-based planning workflows — ideal for sales teams, RevOps, and developers building transparent, data-owned account strategies.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

### Core Platforms (Account Planning & Key Account Management)
- **[Altify](https://altify.com/)**
  Salesforce-native AI-powered account planning, relationship mapping, opportunity mapping, and strategic revenue execution platform.
- **[People.ai](https://www.people.ai/)**
  AI-driven account strategy, automated account plans, dynamic org charts, relationship mapping, and whitespace analysis inside Salesforce.
- **[Uptics](https://uptics.io/)**
  Sales automation CRM with multi-channel outreach, deal pipelines, and account management capabilities for outbound and growth teams.
- **[Membrain](https://www.membrain.com/)**
  B2B growth platform with dedicated Account Growth module for visualizing key accounts, stakeholder influence maps, goal tracking, and expansion planning.
- **[Revegy](https://www.revegy.com/)**
  Strategic sales account planning software with relationship maps, customer strategy maps, whitespace analysis, AI research, and health scorecards.
- **[Strategic Relationship Solutions (SRS)](https://www.srscan.com/)**
  Specialists in complex contracting, partnering, and collaboration management using relational contracting models and structured relationship frameworks.
- **[Salesforce Account Plans](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.account_plans.htm)**
  Native Salesforce Sales Account Plans for capturing customer needs, setting measurable objectives, tracking actions, and visualizing buyer relationship maps.
- **[DemandFarm](https://www.demandfarm.com/)**
  AI-powered key account management and digital account planning suite with white space planners, relationship maps, growth heatmaps, and in-app QBRs.
- **[ClientPoint](https://www.clientpoint.net/)**
  Client relationship workspace platform for secure collaboration, proposal automation, document sharing, and managing high-value client engagements.
- **[Clari](https://www.clari.com/)**
  Revenue orchestration platform with Align mutual action plans, account strategy workspaces, AI-guided execution, and post-sale retention/expansion planning.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**
  Modern open-source CRM (often called the open-source Salesforce alternative) with companies, people, opportunities, custom objects, workflows, and strong foundation for account planning.
- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**
  Highly customizable open-source CRM with accounts, contacts, opportunities, custom entities, relationship linking, and admin-panel extensibility ideal for building account plans.
- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**
  Enterprise-class open-source CRM (SugarCRM fork) with robust account hierarchy, opportunity management, custom modules, and reporting for strategic account workflows.
- **[Odoo CRM](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**
  Full open-source ERP/CRM suite with strong pipeline, opportunity, and account management features that can be extended into structured account planning processes.
- **[ERPNext / Frappe CRM](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**
  Open-source ERP with powerful CRM module supporting accounts, contacts, opportunities, and customizable forms/scripts for key account management.
- **[YetiForce CRM](https://github.com/YetiForceCompany/YetiForceCRM)**
  Feature-rich open-source CRM with 100+ modules, advanced account hierarchy, relationship tracking, and extensive customization for complex B2B planning.
- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**
  Modular open-source ERP/CRM suitable for SMEs, with account/contact management, commercial proposals, and extensible modules for account planning.
- **[Ever Gauzy](https://github.com/ever-co/ever-gauzy)**
  All-in-one open-source platform combining CRM, project management, and HRM — useful for account teams needing integrated relationship and task planning.
- **[Monica](https://github.com/monicahq/monica)**
  Open-source personal relationship manager focused on tracking interactions, reminders, and relationship history (excellent for stakeholder mapping foundations).
- **[Customermates](https://github.com/customermates/customermates)**
  Modern open-source CRM alternative emphasizing contacts, organizations, deals, tasks, n8n automation, and AI-agent workflows.
- **[Relaticle](https://github.com/relaticle/relaticle)** (or search latest mirrors)
  Open-source CRM alternative to HubSpot/Salesforce with contacts, companies, pipelines, custom fields, notes, and multi-workspace support.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- **[Frappe Framework + CRM](https://github.com/frappe/frappe)** and related apps for building fully custom account planning applications.
- **Relationship / Org-chart libraries** and visualization tools (D3.js-based org charts, GraphQL relationship graphs) that integrate with the CRMs above.
- **Open-source mutual action plan / playbook templates** and workflow engines (n8n, Node-RED, Temporal) for execution tracking.
- **LangGraph / AI agent frameworks** combined with CRM data for automated account research, risk detection, and next-best-action recommendations.
- Many community **account hierarchy**, **whitespace analysis notebooks**, and **stakeholder mapping** projects built on top of the major open CRMs.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Twenty** or **EspoCRM** / **SuiteCRM** with **n8n** (or Node-RED), **InfluxDB + Grafana** (for activity metrics), **Ollama** / local LLMs for research summaries, and custom relationship-graph visualizations for intelligent, self-hosted account planning platforms.
